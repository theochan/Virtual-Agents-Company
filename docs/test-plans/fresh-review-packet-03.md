## G03

Source: A packer assembles 24 identical kits. Each kit contains an 18 g component and a 7 g component. Ignore packaging; no other components are included. Find the combined component mass of all kits.

Report: The 18 g components weigh 24 × 18 g = 432 g. The 7 g components weigh 24 × 7 g = 168 g. Their combined mass is 432 g + 168 g = 600 g.

Criterion: Result and workings. The report must explicitly show correct subtotals for each component type and their sum, with gram units. Both the displayed arithmetic and final result must be correct.
